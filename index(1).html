When should I seek medical care?

Go to emergency now: chest pain, trouble breathing, face drooping or slurred speech, heavy bleeding, fainting, severe allergic reaction.

See a doctor within 1-2 days: fever for more than 3 days, repeated vomiting, pain while passing urine, a wound that is getting worse.

Home care is usually fine: mild cold, mild headache, small cuts. Rest, drink water, and watch for changes.

Rural Care

      

      

      

        Basic health guidance
        
Diarrhoea: drink ORS and clean water. Go to a clinic if there is blood, high fever, or no urine for 8 hours.

        
Fever: rest, fluids, light clothes. See a doctor if it lasts over 3 days.

        
Wounds: wash with clean water and soap, cover with a clean cloth. Seek care if deep or very dirty.

        
Pregnancy or infants: always take danger signs to a health centre quickly.
